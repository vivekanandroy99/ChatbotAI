# Brewline Company Guide

Brewline is a specialty coffee company based in Pune, India. Brewline was founded in 2021 by two former baristas, Meera Joshi and Kabir Shah. The company roasts its own beans in small batches every week and sells them through its cafés and its coffee subscription.

## Brewline Cafés

Brewline runs six cafés: four in Pune and two in Mumbai. All Brewline cafés are open every day from 8 AM to 10 PM. The cafés serve coffee, tea, sandwiches and desserts. Brewline cafés do not serve alcohol. Every café has free Wi-Fi and power sockets at most tables, and pets are welcome in the outdoor seating areas.

## Coffee Subscription

The Brewline subscription delivers freshly roasted coffee to your door. There are two plans. The Basic Plan costs Rs 499 per month and includes one 250 gram bag of coffee. The Pro Plan costs Rs 899 per month and includes two 250 gram bags plus early access to limited-edition roasts. Delivery is free on both plans. Subscriptions can be paused or cancelled at any time from the Brewline app, with no cancellation fee. Delivery is currently available only in Pune and Mumbai.

## Brewline App

The Brewline app works on Android and iPhone. With the app you can manage your subscription, order ahead for pickup at any café, and collect Brew Points. Every Rs 100 you spend earns 10 Brew Points, and 200 Brew Points can be exchanged for a free drink.

## Barista Academy

The Brewline Barista Academy teaches coffee-making to beginners. The weekend course runs for two days and costs Rs 3,500, including all equipment and coffee. Classes are held at the Koregaon Park café in Pune and have at most eight students.
