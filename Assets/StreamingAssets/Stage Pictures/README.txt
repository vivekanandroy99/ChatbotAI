Pictures for the stage behind the avatars (menu > Camera & Stage > Backdrop > Picture).
Put .jpg or .png files here - an office, a lobby, brand artwork. Landscape or portrait both work: the picture
fills the screen (cropped, never stretched) and is shown softly out of focus. About 2000-4000 px is plenty.
In a built app this folder is <App>_Data/StreamingAssets/Stage Pictures - pictures can be added there too.
